## 2. Why the state must be a copying/sharing state

[inference] The required question holds a **local linked-marker sharing equivalence class** fixed, then asks whether its allele frequency changes with time, including whether that same state changes in the same direction in different populations. The fitted lower haplotype clusters in Guan's two-layer model are an operational representation of that equivalence class. Upper source labels answer a coarser question: turnover between or within EHG/farmer/etc. An upper label can contain multiple fine lineages whose composition changes. Neither label is a genealogically dated ancestor or MRCA.

[inference] Select the **ELAI/fastPHASE S=1 linked-model limit**, jointly trained on one outcome-blind dictionary, K=5 baseline and preregistered K=3/8 sensitivities. This uses haplotype-copying LD transitions, not independent-marker emissions alone. It is reference-free in the sense of requiring no named ancestral training sources; it is not model-free or truth-free. S=1 preserves the lower sharing-state objective but makes any upper ancestry statistic trivial. It must be tested in the actual executable, not inferred solely from the theoretical limit.

[observation] ELAI's manual documents unphased source/target diploid inputs, but standard `--ps2` writes **S×S upper-state** joint posteriors. Source inspection at the pinned revision confirms `DipInd::compute_ps` allocates `nS*nS`, whereas `compute_pk` allocates `nK` marginal output. Undocumented `--pk1`/`--pk2` parser entries exist, but neither documents or validates the required **joint K×K diploid pair posterior at an excluded virtual site**; `--pk2` has phase/Viterbi wording in commented help. These are not permission to multiply marginals, fabricate phase, or claim the missing interface is solved. Relevant source excerpts are cached byte-for-byte inside JSON evidence wrappers `literature/ELAI_4abc4a8f_{control.cpp,diploid.cpp,diploid.h}.json`, with pinned URLs and original SHA256 values (original source total ~80 KB).

## 3. What is implementable here

[observation] Implemented in-house: preregistration preservation/hashing, streaming historical-fact rechecks, metadata/header counts, core-transcript/feature exclusion assertions, and synthetic algebra tests for the unphased convolution, nonidentifiability, shared-direction statistic and spectral N_eff. **12 tests pass**, including two new runtime-assertion regressions. No genotype array/cache is written and no installed executable/package is added.

[inference] A bounded toy two-layer HMM is computationally conceivable but is **not a defensible AMY prototype in this session**. Diploid inference must integrate chromosome phase and both chains' linked transitions; EM needs normalized/scaled forward–backward, missing-emission handling, reproducible initialization and verified lower-state label alignment. A central virtual-site posterior across the gap must be calibrated. Unphased diploid inference has quadratic state-count cost; restricting markers does not validate emissions, the real transition model, or gap bridging. Writing another convenient independent-SNP HMM would repeat the wrong-model failure. Writing correct recursion still would not recover absent QC/uncertainty, ancestry-matched truth or masked-imputation evidence in ~30 minutes.

[inference] Illustrative sizing, not a performance benchmark: at N=16,339, M=12,133, K=5, a dense all-sample/all-marker ordered lower-pair double array alone would require **39,648,217,400 bytes (~36.9 GiB)**, far outside the disk-output budget. A central-only unordered K=5 posterior is **16,339×15 doubles (~1.87 MiB)**. Authentic inference/export must therefore stream individuals/blocks and retain central posterior summaries, never save the full posterior tensor. Physical disk pressure is real but is not the only scientific blocker; downloading a small binary alone would not satisfy the gates.

## 4. Exact prospective pipeline, including the missing engineering

[observation] Version target: **ELAI source banner 1.01**, official repository `https://github.com/haplotype/ELAI`, pinned source revision **`4abc4a8fcb824d20db2dde672cfaf5c92fd09853`**. The source banner says last update 2021-05-13; README says Linux binary compiled 2021-05-26 and multithreaded binary compiled 2024-03-31. These dates/version banner are recorded distinctly; no binary/source equivalence is asserted. The GitHub metadata and three source files were retrieved using Python HTTPS, not git. No executable was obtained or run. A later approved tool-enabled session must record compiler, executable SHA256, source revision and patch SHA256; an unpinned `main` download is not an exact reproducible version.

[inference] Required pipeline, in this order:

1. **Inputs/QC/provenance.** Start from the existing GRCh37 PGEN/PSAM/PVAR bundle in place; never copy it. Recover original GP/INFO and phase/imputation provenance, or ancestry/technology-matched downsampled truth validating hard calls and propagating their errors. NoQC completeness is not QC. Freeze alleles/orientation, unique biological IDs, dates/uncertainties, countries and kinship crosswalk. Exclude first-/second-degree relatives per preregistration. The structural outcome is the measured two unordered AMY haplotype classes, not an imputed SNP surrogate.
2. **Mask before preprocessing.** Exclude the entire minimum AMY interval from any new inference/imputation/phase input; assert transcript coverage, all feature positions and variant-index intersections at every execution. Tested AMY structures/copy number, dates and populations are never fitting emissions. Require masked preprocessing or ancestry-matched truth demonstrating absence of focal-information leakage. Independent truth/LD screening must show the retained flanks do not violate the preregistered leakage requirement; otherwise stop and expand the exclusion prospectively. The 412 kb coordinate mask alone cannot promise all LD neighbours were removed.
3. **Reference/dictionary construction.** Train jointly on all eligible outcome-blind panel individuals (one biological person one unit), using S=1, the preregistered linked flank panels and K/seeds. Named source panels are **not used to anchor the primary sharing states**. Keep five curated broad AADR source brackets only for the frozen heldout structure-calibration gate: each must have ≥2 heldouts and adequate quality after exclusion. They are proxy validation labels, not lower-state genealogy truth. Preserve ancient/modern/technology balance diagnostics and assess modern-donor dominance.
4. **Unphased input, no invented phasing source.** Convert validated hard allele-pair calls in bounded streaming BIMBAM shards, sorted positions; missing genotypes remain missing. Use two-allele genotype syntax without the manual's phased `=` declaration. Targets and sources need **no external phasing** for this ELAI lane. Phased ancestry-matched truth is required separately for simulation/accuracy calibration and is currently missing; the unordered AMY H1/H2 labels are not a substitute. Dosage rounding or manufacturing GL from dosage is forbidden. Stock ELAI does not ingest arbitrary GP/GL directly; use only the calibrated hard-call/error-propagation lane or stop.
5. **Authentic model execution and exporter.** Compile the pinned source only in a later approved session. Verify S=1/no-source behavior on enumerated linked diplotypes. Use exact quadratic diploid inference, not the manual's less-accurate linear warm-up as final inference. Fit 50 EM steps, then 20-step convergence check with saved parameters; K=5/3/8, seeds 20261009/10/11. The stock training-command skeleton for a single unphased cohort is `elai-lin -g cohort.inp -p 1 -pos flanks.pos -C 1 -c 5 -w 0 -s 50 -R 20261009 -o amy_s1_k5`. This is **not a working fine-state pipeline** and has not been tested: a source modification must retain a missing-emission virtual marker at x*=104121000, audit switches across the masked gap, and export calibrated ordered lower pairs marginalized over upper states and converted to unordered q(a,b). Do not use options that drop the all-missing virtual marker. Modify the real `DipInd::forward_backward_diploid` posterior computation/export rather than attach a hand-built window HMM; verify normalization, symmetry and tiny-case enumeration. The patch/central-site exporter remains unimplemented, with no invented CLI flag. Audit/suppress stock whole-marker output, which otherwise breaks the budget.
6. **Bound writes.** Stream PGEN reads and BIMBAM writes; genotype shards ≤200 MB, every file <500 MB, all new output <2 GB. For the baseline 12,133 markers, 512-person shards at three bytes/call are ~18.6 MB each, ~595 MB total (headers/IDs extra). Do not emit all-marker K×K posterior files; central q only. For wider sensitivities, compute exact encoded sizes first, reuse/read existing inputs where supported, otherwise process/delete our own shards sequentially. Check `df -h /` before any large write and enforce counters before each write. If source input/output behavior cannot meet these bounds, stop rather than silently subsample/change the preregistration.
7. **Gates before outcomes.** Run all preregistered structure, central-gap truth, calibrated posterior, seed/window/K stability, coverage, forbidden-data perturbation, neutral/turnover/error and power gates; report numbers first. Many null/power simulations are required: this is a tool-validation project, not a one-command rescue. Missing or failed assignment/leakage gate means STOP, no AMY statistic.
8. **Conditional temporal likelihood, only after gates.** Implement the registered ASAFE-type extension, marginalizing the **joint pair posterior inside** the genotype likelihood, integrating ages/measurement error and testing profile identifiability. Estimate each state/country contrast from 6,000 to 4,000 BP, with support in both fixed endpoint bins. The same state is shared only through the common dictionary and minimum C≥20 chromosomes/H≥10 individuals per bin in ≥2 countries. Compute per-state conservative common-direction T; use joint drift-only null covariance for the **spectral N_eff of shared-state scores**, not raw countries. Local N_eff≥2 and joint calibration are necessary for an independent-replication interpretation.

[inference] **Honest feasibility verdict:** no currently implementable and validated in-house method meets the requested fine-state constraints. The exact prospective baseline is pinned; missing engineering/data/validation are specified rather than represented as a ready tool pipeline. Authentic ELAI lower-joint/virtual-site support plus QC/truth is needed. The off-the-shelf upper `--ps2` lane could be developed for **source-conditioned** frequencies using adequate ancient references, but would change the question. For genuinely genealogical ancestors, validated phase followed by local trees is a different substantial project. Neither alternative is silently adopted.

## 5. Reference/source panel question

[observation] **1KG EUR is an imputation/donor resource, not an EHG or Iranian ancestral truth set.** A modern EUR donor may improve imputation while also pulling low-coverage ancient genotypes toward modern haplotypes. The earlier 16 EHG/7 Iranian raw-call panel had inadequate Iranian validation support; replacing it with modern EUR does not create historical labels.

[inference] The primary reference-free lower dictionary avoids claiming an exhaustive named ancient source set, but still risks learning technology/coverage/modern-reference structure. Its known-structure gate uses independent heldout ancient labels and its central-gap truth gate needs ancestry-matched haplotypes/mosaics. For an upper-source alternative, reconstruct quality-checked ancient WHG/EHG/farmer/Iranian/steppe proxy panels with harmonized allele sets, date/geography documentation, sufficient independent heldouts and leave-one-population-out validation; do not declare 7 sparse Iranian individuals sufficient by fiat. Neither the current broad labels nor 100% imputed calls certify local truth.

## 7. Detection/identifiability and interpretation limits

[inference] The registered ideal single-state two-bin floor at 225 independent diploid outcomes, p=.5, is Δ≈**0.1321** over 2 kyr (≈**0.0660/kyr**). Splitting outcomes by state and country, bridge abstentions, relatedness and errors worsens power. The actual local floor is unavailable until calibration on frozen support. The old global-history floor cannot be transferred to local strata. Insufficient fixed-bin support must yield “no evaluable replication,” not relocated endpoints.

[inference] Even perfect unphased diploid genotypes may not identify a named state's direction: an exclusively AB-paired cohort cannot distinguish (p_A,p_B)=(.9,.1) from (.1,.9). Pair-composition diversity/homo-state anchors, bounded profile intervals, multistart mode checks and the registered information gate are necessary. Arbitrary priors/regularization do not manufacture biological identification. Upper dosage is insufficient to assign heterozygous alleles, and H1/H2 names do not phase AMY onto flanking chromosomes.

[inference] Pitfalls carried forward from the study: weak inter-method local agreement despite global agreement; ancient source imbalance and domain shift; missing quality-dependent imputation errors; date-dependent state misassignment creating false conditional trends; EM starting/switch nonidentifiability; state splits/label switching across runs; long exclusion gaps crossing recombination boundaries; linked-flank/imputation leakage despite coordinate masking; very fine states making the allele invariant; nonlinear time paths and sampling turnover; drift/migration within declared states; and covariance between countries/states carrying the same lineage. A lower cluster has no inferred MRCA date, and agreement in several countries is not several independent mutation origins.

[inference] Passing the design would support **conditional-change prioritization**, not proof of selection or Akbari over-correction. Neutral finer-lineage turnover, linked selection and measurement error remain alternative explanations unless separately rejected. Mies & Mathieson's “within-background change OR underestimated ancestry shifts” distinction is precisely why neither the released AMY max X nor coarse-state adjustment settles this estimand.
