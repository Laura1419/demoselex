- [inference] Block labels use the maximum absolute Akbari X among **all scored variants in that block**, not only the representative: strong if |X| > 5.45; moderate if |X| >= 3.16 (includes strong). ## Prediction and falsification

[speculation] The top-100 block list will have strong-label enrichment >1 relative to all scored blocks, and at least one of the fixed canonical genes will overlap or be the assigned nearest gene of a top-100 block. Either absence falsifies the corresponding prediction; failure is informative and will not be hidden. Shared labels make even success descriptive, not independent validation. Novel candidates are follow-up hypotheses, not newly selected loci.

## Expected false positives and structural limits

[inference] Drift/jitter can score highly; carriage and deme turnover can confound changes; MHC/long LD and adjacent tiles can multiply one signal; reuse of Akbari genotypes/labels inflates apparent agreement. Tile maxima also favor densely scored tiles. Every top-N non-significant tile consumes follow-up effort but is NOT proven false-positive. No calibrated block-level false-discovery claim is planned.
