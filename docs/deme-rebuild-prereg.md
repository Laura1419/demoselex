## Drift-plus-jump test fixed before run

[inference] For each SNP and deme/node, estimate frequency as mean called dosage/2, with conservative effective chromosome count = number of called individuals (pseudo-haploid calls are not two independent chromosomes). Locus support: >=8 called people in each node, >=3 supported nodes; require support in ALL qualifying demes for the joint test. Deme slope is sample-size-weighted linear regression on forward time (-BP/1,000); report slope and fitted change over its own span.

[inference] Null: Gaussian small-change frequency diffusion plus zero-mean deme-specific admixture jumps. For adjacent nodes, standardized frequency increments have variance a*delta_generations + b*genetic_centroid_distance_squared + c, plus observed binomial sampling variance. Estimate nonnegative a,b,c on training control increments by NNLS moment regression after subtracting sampling variance, delta_generations=delta_years/29; Ne=1/(2a). This does not separately identify migration and Ne when predictors are collinear: report fitted components and conditioning. b*dPC^2+c is the jump second moment, not an inferred ancestry proportion. Estimate cross-deme slope correlation from training controls; shrink halfway to identity to avoid singular estimates. Construct per-deme slope variance from the full cumulative random-walk covariance (including shared-node sampling covariance). Test equal-weight sum of standardized deme slopes, standardized by that correlation; record sign count separately. Two-sided SNP P from the Gaussian law; AMY's increase direction is prespecified, but no AMY process P unless copy-number process variance can be independently calibrated. Do not convert SNP Ne into copy-number variance without a haplotype model.

[inference] Draw 1,800 fixed-seed held-out background sites, chromosome 1 and 6 excluded entirely from the control test; use first 900 for fitting and last 900 as independent validation. Filter MAF in [.1,.9] and complete support; do not select on slopes or Akbari X. They are background, not guaranteed neutral. Controls matched by the same support/time design and allele-frequency standardization. Record held-out pass fraction P<=.05 and exact binomial 95% interval. Calibration gate: 95% interval contains .05 AND held-out N>=100. If it fails, interpretation is BLOCKED; no post-hoc rank transform may be called proof of process calibration. Any revised model must receive a timestamped preregistration amendment before its run. Biological SNP discovery is withheld in this short session even if calibration passes.

[inference] Before interpreting AMY, report effective count from training slope correlation, achievable conditional power from two-sided N(shift,1) for common per-deme standardized effects .25,.5,1,2, and fair-sign unanimity tails for D=2,3,4,5,6,10. Fair iid signs imply two-sided 2^(1-D), not under arbitrary demographic migration. Power is conditional on the fitted model and does not establish demographic identifiability.
