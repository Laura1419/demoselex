# AMY binarized preregistration

## NEW ANALYSIS — written before results, 2026-10-09

[observation] This local preregistration was written before running any binarized fits, frequency summaries, power calculation, modern comparison or full-|X| distribution analysis. It preserves all earlier authors' conclusions. Existing per-haplotype results are known; this is not a blinded hypothesis. Runtime cap approximately 35 minutes, with incremental artifacts.

### Exact binary allele and sensitivity (a MODELING CHOICE)

[observation] PRIMARY: A = H1^a (the released one-AMY1-copy shortest structural state); B = every other labelled haplotype. The names observed in the release are H1^a, H2A0, H2A2B2, H3A2, H3A3B3, H3^r, H4A2, H4A2B2, H5, H7, H9. Parse the integer after initial H as haploid AMY1 copy count; A/B does NOT use AMY2A/AMY2B counts. Missing/unknown labels are excluded, never recoded as A. Calling H1^a ancestral follows the paper's structural framing, not an independently inferred ancestral sequence.

[inference] The A/B cut is a **MODELING CHOICE**, not a naturally biallelic locus. Pooling longer states pools distinct mutational histories. Sensitivity 1: A = all haplotypes with <=2 AMY1 copies (H1^a, H2A0, H2A2B2); sensitivity 2: A = all with <=3 copies (add H3A2, H3A3B3, H3^r). B is the complement in each case. Do not select a cut after seeing its statistic. Check name-derived copies against measured mean diploid AMY1/2 among homozygotes per label, with n; also fit additive label-count least squares to all measured diplotypes to estimate per-haplotype CN, stating rank and confounding. No genotype recoding based on that check.

### Nulls, two channels and power — before trajectory interpretation

[observation] Seed 20261009. Attempt 199 permutations per cut/model for each of (i) whole dosages shuffled within p2 ancestry, (ii) unrestricted shuffle; refit variance components. Empirical two-sided P=(1+#|Z_perm|>=|Z_obs|)/(1+successful draws); report attempts, failures, |Z| quantiles. The conditional null retains composition and is known not to detect LCT (previous P=.965); it cannot by itself reject known selection. These are exchangeability diagnostics, not calibrated drift/kinship nulls.

[observation] Reuse mixture_null.fit/design and the two-channel definitions at individual allele-frequency y=dosage/2, forward t=-BP/1000: beta_within = group-centred time slope with HC3 SE; beta_between = pooled time slope of fitted group allele means; beta_total = their sum (an index, NOT the observed pooled slope). Also report pooled slope and exact additive pooled within contribution. Member-stratified bootstrap 1000 draws gives between SE. Mostly carriage means >=75% of absolute pooled slope contributions lies in between; mostly within means >=75% within; otherwise both. Opposing signs must be disclosed. Group-fixed-effect correction removes carriage algebraically; report whether residual within is detectable instead of conflating this with GRM covariance correction.

[observation] Detection floor is reported BEFORE interpretation: 80%-power, two-sided alpha=.05 approximate MDE=(t_.975,df + normal_.8)*HC3 SE for the within slope, in B-frequency/kyr, plus 2kyr and observed date-span contrasts. Also calculate ideal independent-binomial floor using observed group p_B: SE=sqrt(sum(x_i^2*p_g*(1-p_g)/2))/sum(x_i^2). This is an optimistic assay/design floor (diploid independence, no kinship/drift); HC3 is an asymptotic Wald approximation, not a demographic power calibration. Compare observed |within slope| and its 2kyr contrast to both floors. A null below floor is underpowered, not evidence of no selection.

### Decision rule

[inference] Visible directional signal means a positive forward pooled B-frequency slope with unrestricted permutation P<=.05 (descriptive component progression is separately counted). Direct cutoff classifications use |X-equivalent|>3.16 and >5.45; both anchored comparisons are shown and not promoted to original-paper measurements. GRM removal requires an on/off |Z| ratio <=.5 with off passing a reference cutoff and on failing it; otherwise report quantitative attenuation. Within-history visibility requires within HC3 P<=.05 and a slope above the reported approximate 80%-power floor; lack of either is not proof of absence. Robustness means directional classification and correction conclusion agree across all three prespecified cuts; disclose changes. No result is used to invent a new cutoff or weaken this rule.
